# Bild-Prompts (Partner-Seite + Startseite)

Style for every image (append to each prompt):
"Photorealistic editorial photo, natural light, calm premium mood, navy and warm champagne colour accents, shallow depth of field, Germany, no text, no logos, no handshake, no keys, no stock-photo smiles, no watermark."
Faces: adults, neutral calm expression, not looking at camera. Label as "Beispielbild" on site.

## Partner page (4)
1. HERO (16:9) – "Kompetenzen verbinden. Zusammenarbeit gestalten."
   Several professionals (property manager, agent, advisor) standing around a large table with a district map and floor plans in a bright German office, seen from behind/side, calm discussion. Meaning: different experts working together.
2. CARD "Welche Partner angesprochen werden" (4:5)
   Three professionals in business-casual in front of a German apartment building, one holding a tablet with a unit list, one with a folder. Meaning: real-estate companies, property managers, advisors.
3. CARD "Was ARVENTAS ergänzt" (3:4)
   Two people at a table: one hands over a closed navy folder, the other reviews a floor plan and an investor profile sheet; a quiet German street through the window. Meaning: access, analysis, matching, personal support added to the partner's work.
4. CARD "Wie Zuständigkeiten geklärt werden" (wide 16:7)
   Two people on opposite sides of a table, one pointing at a role chart / clearly separated sheets (left/right), pen in hand, notebook open. Meaning: roles and interfaces are agreed upfront.

## Home (4)
5. "Zugang" (strength 1)  – Immobilien abseits öffentlicher Portale
   An adult person walking up to the discreet entrance door of a quiet German Altbau in the evening, seen from behind, warm light inside. Meaning: private, non-public access.
6. "Analyse" (strength 2) – Wert, Potenzial, Risiken nachvollziehbar einordnen
   Close-up of hands with a pencil on a facade drawing and a valuation sheet with neutral bar charts (no numbers), German apartment block model blurred behind. Meaning: structured, traceable assessment.
7. "Abwicklung" (strength 3) – Persönlich begleitet zur fundierten Entscheidung
   Advisor and client sit side by side at a notary-style table in a calm office, reviewing documents together, a German street outside. Meaning: personal guidance to a sound decision.
8. "Wissen" – Hinter die Fassade schauen.
   A person on a street looking up at a German apartment building facade (from behind, small in frame), golden hour. Meaning: looking behind the facade.
